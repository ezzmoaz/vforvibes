About

V for Vibes is a two-player game box built around iPhone Duo's hinge. Stand the phone on the table in a V, sit on opposite sides, and each of you sees only your half. That one trick makes hidden-information games possible on a single phone.

The app reads the hinge angle live, splits its layout exactly on the crease, guides players into the V and pauses the moment it opens flat. Everything is sized to be read from across the table.

The games

  • Vault — a two-person heist. One of you is at the vault, the other has the blueprints. Only talking gets you in.
  • Decoy — say it without saying it. One describes, the other picks the answer out of look-alikes.
  • Sketch Duel — the same picture for both, sixty seconds, closest copy wins.
  • Quick Draw — your whole half is one giant button. Fastest finger wins.
