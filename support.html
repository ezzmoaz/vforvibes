Setting up

Foldable iPhone: open it, stand it on the table in a V between you and sit on opposite sides. Vault and Decoy play in the V; Sketch Duel and Quick Draw play with the phone open flat. Games play on the inner screen — the cover asks you to open the phone.

Any other iPhone or iPad: lay it flat between you. In portrait the screen splits top and bottom, with the far half turned to face the other player.

Common questions

The game paused on its own.

Folding the phone, opening a V flat mid-round, or leaving the app pauses every game so nobody loses a round. Stand it back up (or come back) and tap Resume.

How do I restore V for Vibes Plus?

Tap Plus on the home screen, then Restore Purchases. Plus is a one-time purchase and works with Family Sharing, so everyone in your family group gets it.

What does Plus include?

The Veteran and Nightmare vaults, Sketch Duel's Memory mode and more picture packs, Decoy's Normal and Hard modes and more decks, and every new pack we add. All four games are free to play without it.

We can see each other's half.

In Vault and Decoy, part of the fun is not peeking. On a foldable, the V hides each half; on a flat phone or iPad it's on the honour system.

The text is hard to read from across the table.

The app follows your Dynamic Type setting — raise it in Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size and every screen grows.
