V for Vibes

Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

The short version: V for Vibes does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything the app knows stays on your device.

This policy covers the V for Vibes app for iPhone and iPad, made by Moaaz Belal ("we").

Data we collect

None. The app does not send any personal or usage data to us or to anyone else. It contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code.

What stays on your device

To work, the app keeps a few things in its own storage on your device only:

  • the player names you type in (optional);
  • game records such as best times and streaks, and your last-used game settings;
  • whether you have seen the introduction and the rules cards.

We never receive this information. Deleting the app deletes it.

Motion and the hinge

The app reads the phone's motion sensors and, on foldable iPhones, the hinge angle, only to tell whether the phone is standing in a V, lying flat or folded. These readings are used in the moment and are never recorded or sent anywhere.

Purchases

V for Vibes Plus is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we don't receive your payment details or your Apple Account information. Apple's own privacy policy applies to those purchases.

Sharing

Some results can be turned into a picture you may share. The picture is made on your device and only goes where you choose to send it using the standard share sheet.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
